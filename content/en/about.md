## Dmitry Verkhoturov

I'm a Site Reliability Engineer based in Amsterdam, but currently living in London. My work centres on building and maintaining high-availability systems — making sure infrastructure is reliable, observable, and doesn't wake anyone up at night.

## What I write about

This blog covers topics I deal with professionally and personally:

- **Site Reliability Engineering** — monitoring, system design, incident management, and the ever-blurry line between SRE and DevOps
- **Technology** — tools, self-hosting, open source projects
- **Life** — adventures beyond the keyboard, including skydiving and motogymkhana

## Open source

I contribute to several open source projects, most notably [Remark42](https://remark42.com) — a privacy-focused, self-hosted comment engine. You can see it in action at the bottom of every post on this blog.

My other projects and contributions are on [GitHub](https://github.com/paskal).
